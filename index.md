Privacy Policy — CoinPulse

  Last updated: April 13, 2026

  CoinPulse ("the extension") is a browser extension that displays cryptocurrency prices and lets you maintain a personal
  watchlist and price alerts. This policy explains what data the extension handles.

  Data we collect

  None. CoinPulse does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics,
  no trackers, and no advertising.

  Data stored on your device

  The extension stores the following in your browser's local storage, on your device only:

  - Your selected coin, theme, layout, and currency preferences
  - Your watchlist (up to 5 coin identifiers)
  - Your price alert thresholds

  This data never leaves your browser. Uninstalling the extension, or clearing the extension's storage, permanently deletes
  it.

  Third-party services

  To display prices, the extension makes requests to the public CoinGecko API (https://api.coingecko.com). These requests
  contain only the coin identifiers you are viewing or watching. No personal identifiers are sent. CoinGecko's privacy
  policy is available at https://www.coingecko.com/en/privacy.

  Permissions

  - Notifications — used only to deliver price alerts that you configure yourself. No marketing notifications are ever sent.
  - Host access to api.coingecko.com — used only to fetch public market data for coins you select.

  Remote code

  The extension does not load or execute any remote code. All code shipped with the extension is contained in the published
  package.

  Children

  CoinPulse is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

  Changes to this policy

  If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same URL with a new "Last updated" date.
